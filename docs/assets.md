# PLAYER MARKET — Asset and Texture Registry

All graphical assets, icons, and textures in PLAYER MARKET are custom-designed, licensed under MIT, and adhere to a unified obsidian-graphite-emerald financial platform design system.

## External Open-Source Asset Attributions (CC0 / MIT)
In addition to custom pixel-art UI frames and shaders, the terminal textures incorporate and adapt open-source assets under permissive MIT and CC0 licenses:
1. **`fanquanpp/pixel-vault`** (MIT License):
   - Adapted into crisp 16x16 currency coin (`currency_cr.png`), faceted cyan gem (`currency_diamond.png`, `tab_upgrades.png`), and biometric/skull emblems (`tab_blackmarket.png`, `tab_buyback.png`).
2. **`GUI 77` Cyberpunk Icon Set** (MIT License):
   - Adapted into communication envelope (`tab_mail.png`) and security padlocks (`lock_closed.png`, `lock_open.png`), paired with a crisp transparent cyber-globe (`tab_news.png`).
3. **`Kenney RPG Extension`** (CC0 1.0 Public Domain):
   - Layered beige parchment frames (`panel_beigeLight.png` and `panelInset_beige.png`) with a custom wax seal to compose `textures/gui/parchment_contract.png`.
4. **`Gold Treasure Icons 16x16` & `Basic RPG Icons`** (CC0 1.0 Public Domain):
   - Adapted for financial ledger and commodity resource tab iconography (`tab_resources.png`, `tab_contracts.png`, `tab_portfolio.png`, `tab_sell.png`).
5. **`Mekanism` Dark UI Reference & Custom 9-Slice Sprites** (MIT License / Custom):
   - Hard-pixel 9-slice GUI components (`textures/gui/sprites/*.png` + `.mcmeta`): crisp bordered `panel.png`, `header.png`, `sidebar.png`, `footer.png`, `hud_bg.png`, `card*.png`, recessed `18x18` item frames `slot_dark.png` & `slot_dark_hover.png`, unified `32x16` button set (`btn_*[_hover].png`), `pill*.png`, and segmented progress bars (`bar_*.png`).

## Palette Standards
- **Obsidian Black**: `#0A0D14` (Window canvases, backdrop fill)
- **Dark Graphite**: `#141923` (Interactive panel, nine-slice cards)
- **Card Divider**: `#1E2638` (Thin borders and section bounding)
- **Emerald Green**: `#10B981` (Primary financial accents, CR currency, success states)
- **Muted Platinum**: `#94A3B8` (Telemetry descriptions, secondary captions)
- **Amber Warning**: `#F59E0B` (Leases, deadlines, macroeconomic news)
- **Restrained Crimson**: `#EF4444` (Sanctions, liquidated stats, blocked state)
