# PLAYER MARKET — Asset and Texture Registry

All graphical assets, icons, and textures in PLAYER MARKET are custom-designed, licensed under MIT, and adhere to a unified obsidian-graphite-emerald financial platform design system.

## Palette Standards
- **Obsidian Black**: `#0A0D14` (Window canvases, backdrop fill)
- **Dark Graphite**: `#141923` (Interactive panel, nine-slice cards)
- **Card Divider**: `#1E2638` (Thin borders and section bounding)
- **Emerald Green**: `#10B981` (Primary financial accents, CR currency, success states)
- **Muted Platinum**: `#94A3B8` (Telemetry descriptions, secondary captions)
- **Amber Warning**: `#F59E0B` (Leases, deadlines, macroeconomic news)
- **Restrained Crimson**: `#EF4444` (Sanctions, liquidated stats, blocked state)
